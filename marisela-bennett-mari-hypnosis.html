About Marisela

A path of lasting growth

Originally from Las Vegas, Nevada, I lived in Michigan for 14 years before relocating to Northern California. Outside of work, I enjoy exploring nature with my husband and three kids.

After discovering hypnosis five years ago and experiencing lasting personal and professional growth, I became a clinical hypnotherapist dedicated to helping others achieve their full potential.

I help clients create positive change in the subconscious mind through calm, secure guided imagery, allowing them to accept their own suggestions to reach their goals.
